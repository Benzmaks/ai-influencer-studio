## Rendering fingerprint

**At 10× inspection (inspection goal, not a claimed real magnification factor):**
- Skin has extremely fine *uneven* follicle/pore structures; some patches smoother and some more textured, respecting anatomical region and focus.
- A thin natural oil film catches sunlight in extremely small interrupted specular highlights, with **underlying pores still discernible in highlight regions** and without repeating rings, artificial wrinkles or uniform embossing.
- Heat-related sweat is sparse and physically plausible: rare micro-beads of differing sizes, not repeated large droplets or a uniformly wet face.
- Very fine peach fuzz, individual brow/lash hairs, tiny lip lines and subtle pigment transitions follow anatomy; avoid artificially dark freckle overlays.
- Natural upper teeth geometry, subdued ivory tone and ordinary gum-tooth transitions; no extra short lower teeth, duplicated rows, synthetic-perfect whiteness.
- Cheek moves with smile: realistic gradual folds and slightly narrowed eyes; no engraved wrinkles between eye and nose.

**At regular camera distance:** tiny pores and sweat appropriately collapse into coherent mid-frequency tone and microcontrast. Do not scale up pore/freckle size to make them visible. Grain and optical softness depend on camera profile.

## Composition / scene template

Smiling subject seated outdoors at a coffee shop; candid body language, one hand optionally supporting cheek, a cup and subdued urban background. Sunlit highlights should be bright but structured, not lacquered. A unique beauty mark and characteristic smile survive through scene variants. For macro inspections, crop forehead/eye/nose/cheek/lips, not just skin swatches.

## Prompt nucleus (editable, not validated)

"Use the approved adult fictional character with a consistent subtle cheek beauty mark and a natural asymmetric smile. Preserve anatomically coherent eyelids, cheeks, jaw, gums and upper teeth. Warm skin shows microscopic heterogeneous pores, fine facial vellus hairs, faint nonuniform pigmentation and subtle local sheen. Even in the brightest nose, cheek and forehead reflections, the skin retains microstructure; oil appears as broken, tiny highlights, with a handful of plausible micro-sweat beads rather than wet lacquer. Facial marks are sparse and anchored, never plastered-on. Camera detail adapts naturally to viewing distance; no artificial sharpened pore overlay, ring-like texture, or plastic smoothing."

## Acceptance checks

- [ ] Facial mark remains stable in position, shape and relative scale.
- [ ] Smile with upper teeth looks believable; eyes and cheeks respond naturally.
- [ ] Skin colour and texture are rendered accurately across changed light and distinct skin tones without using stereotypes.
